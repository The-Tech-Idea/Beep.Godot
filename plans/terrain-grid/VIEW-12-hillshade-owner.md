# VIEW-12 — Hillshade has one owner; the tile view draws it

**Type:** fix (rule 3/7: a second shade computation; a generated field nothing reads) + enhancement (relief in the tile view) · **Area:** `TerrainVisualSnapshot`, `TerrainPaintedRendererComponent` (`WriteShadeTexel`, `BuildMaps`, `UpdateMaps`), `ITerrainSurfaceData`, `TerrainShadingStage` (a writer for edits), new `TerrainShadeMap`, `terrain_tile_detail.gdshader`, `TerrainTileRendererComponent.ApplyGroundDetail` · **Status:** proposed 2026-09-15 · **Effort:** S (1–2 days) · **Risk:** low–medium (a **look change** in the painted view; ships with before/after captures for the owner's approval)

## Gap

1. **Generation owns shade; the painter recomputes it.** `TerrainShadingStage.Apply` (`TerrainShadingStage.cs:26-53`) lights the elevation gradient scaled by the recipe's `HillshadeStrength` (`:30-31`, `:50`; zero = flat), `TerrainTileReductionStage.cs:113` averages it per cell, and the handoff writes it to every cell as `terrain_shade` (`GridCellDataComponent.cs:634`, `:647`). The painted view's live path ignores that value: `WriteShadeTexel` (`TerrainPaintedRendererComponent.cs:516-531`) computes `TerrainShadingStage.AtCell(_elevationField, …)` (`:522-523`), whose strength is hard-coded `1f` (`:56-63`, `:62`), while its generator-preview path reads the field's shade (`:524`). The same map shades differently in a live scene (the lab, every game) and in a generator-only demo; the recipe's `HillshadeStrength` reaches one of the two; and `terrain_shade` in the cells is read by nobody. `TerrainVisualSnapshot.Sample` carries `Elevation`, not `Shade` (`TerrainVisualSnapshot.cs:8-9`, `:71-73`), and `_elevationField` (`:440`, `:508`) exists only to feed the recompute.
2. **The 2026-09-06 fix dialled down the copy.** `plans/TERRAIN_VIEW_GRID_INTEGRATION.md:612-619` records that "full-strength cell-gradient hillshade caused most broad cloudy contrast in the lab capture" and lowered the painted `ShadeStrength` to 0.35 (`TerrainPaintedRendererComponent.cs:73`; `terrain_splat.gdshader:57`) — a display gain on the renderer's own computation, not the generation dial that owns the fact. FEAT-05's terraforming plans to "recompute `terrain_shade` … with `TerrainShadingStage.AtCell`" after an edit (`FEAT-05-terraforming-and-ramps.md`, design 1), which only reaches the screen if the painter reads the key.
3. **The tile view has no relief shading at all.** `terrain_tile_detail.gdshader` (`:1-19`) modulates by ground-texture luminance only; `ApplyGroundDetail` (`TerrainTileRendererComponent.cs:395-415`) binds `ground_texture`, `cell_size`, `repeat_cells`, `detail_strength`. Relief in Game tiles is a z-raise; a hill and a plain are the same green.

## Design

1. **The cell value is the one owner.** `ITerrainSurfaceData` (`ITerrainSurfaceData.cs:5-12`) gains `ShadeAtCell(cell)`: `LiveTerrainSurfaceData` reads `terrain_shade` (default 1), `GeneratedTerrainField` returns its reduced per-cell shade; `Sample` gains `float Shade` (`ReadSample`, `:71-73`); `WriteShadeTexel` becomes `lighting = water ? 1 : shade` on both paths; `_elevationField` and the painter's `AtCell` call are deleted. `shade_strength` stays what it is — a per-view display gain over the owned value — and the recipe's `HillshadeStrength` is the only place the relief contrast is *decided*.
2. **`AtCell` keeps a consumer: the edit writer.** `TerrainShadingStage.Refresh(GridCellDataComponent cells, Vector2I cell)` recomputes the 3×3 neighbourhood from `terrain_elevation` with `AtCell` (scaled by the recipe strength the world component carries) and writes `terrain_shade`; VIEW-09's elevation commit calls it (today's only elevation writer), FEAT-05's `GridTerrainEditComponent` calls it later. The painter's chunk-window update (`UpdateMaps`, `:459-484`) already rewrites the shade texels of changed chunks plus a one-cell halo.
3. **One shade-map builder, two views.** `TerrainShadeMap` (`ecs/terrain/`): builds the linear-filtered R8 texture from `ShadeAtCell` over a window (the construction at `:435`, `:450`, `:453`, `:478-479` moves here; the halving convention "0.7..1.3 halved into a byte, doubled in the shader", `:520-526`, is its contract), with `Update(rect)` for chunk windows. The painter binds it as today's `shade_map` (`terrain_splat.gdshader:25`). `terrain_tile_detail.gdshader` gains `shade_map` (`filter_linear, repeat_disable`), `map_origin`, `map_size`, `shade_strength = 0.35`, and multiplies `COLOR.rgb` by `mix(1.0, shade * 2.0, shade_strength)` next to the detail term (`:18`); `ApplyGroundDetail` binds it on **every** biome layer — a material is created whenever a shade map exists, with `detail_strength = 0` where a layer has no ground texture (today `Material = null`, `:398-402`). Live edits: the tile view's cell-change path calls `TerrainShadeMap.Update` for the 3×3 (texture recreated, never `ImageTexture.Update` — headless rule).
4. The block view and the autotile view are lit by their art and their profiles (VIEW-09); no shade map there, stated in the docs rather than left as a gap.
5. **Look change — owner's approval.** Reading the generated shade changes the painted view on every live map (the lab included): the value is `HillshadeStrength`-scaled where the recompute was unit-strength. Before landing, capture the lab's Painted and Game-tiles views on seeds 31415 and 4242 at two zooms into `tests/output/painted_shading/` before and after, and present them to Fahad; land only on his approval (decision of 2026-09-15). `terrain_painted_shading_probe.gd` (`:26` default 0.35, `:53-54` spans) is re-baselined once against the accepted defaults.
6. Docs: `docs/terrain-engine/TerrainShadingStage.md`, `TerrainPaintedRendererComponent.md`, `TerrainTileRendererComponent.md`, and the owner statement in `plans/TERRAIN_VIEW_GRID_INTEGRATION.md`'s shading note.

## Guards (fail first)

- Headless: an 8×4 live map with an elevation ramp (as `terrain_painted_shading_probe.gd:17-20`) and explicit `terrain_shade` values written in a 0.8/1.2 pattern → the painter's `shade_map` texels equal `round(shade × 0.5 × 255)` per cell; a generator-preview painter on the same field equals the live painter byte for byte. **Mutation:** restore the `AtCell` recompute in `WriteShadeTexel` → texels follow the gradient, not the written values → fails.
- GPU (`SubViewport` capture, the pattern of `terrain_painted_shading_probe.gd:9-16`, `:44-52`): Game tiles on a live map with a NW-facing and a SE-facing slope under one grass atlas → the NW slope's mean red exceeds the SE slope's by more than 2 %; with `shade_strength = 0` they are equal. **Mutation:** skip the `shade_map` bind in `ApplyGroundDetail` → equal → fails.
- `TerrainShadingStage.Refresh` after an elevation change → the 3×3 `terrain_shade` values change and the painter's texels follow through the chunk window. **Mutation:** skip `Refresh` → texels unchanged → fails.
- Pin: `TerrainShadingStage.AtCell(` not called from `TerrainPaintedRendererComponent.cs`; `_elevationField` absent from it. **Mutation:** re-add → fails.
- `painted_shading` re-baselined once; `painted_blend`, `shader_alignment`, `terrain_world_live_source_probe` green.

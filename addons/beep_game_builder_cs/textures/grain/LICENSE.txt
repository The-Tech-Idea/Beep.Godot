These are used as LUMINANCE MASKS by KitGrain -- modulated into the active
palette's plate colour rather than drawn as art -- so they carry no colour of
their own and reskin with the theme.

Selected by measurement, not by eye: see tools/genre_shapes/pick_grain.py.

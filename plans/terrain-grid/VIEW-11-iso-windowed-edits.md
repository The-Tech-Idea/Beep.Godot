# VIEW-11 — Isometric block view: windowed live edits

**Type:** enhancement (edit cost of a shipped view) · **Area:** `TerrainIsometricRendererComponent` (`OnCellChanged`, `OnCellsChangedSignal`, `Rebuild`, `MeasureSummitFloor`, `ShowsSide`, seabed, rivers) · **Status:** proposed 2026-09-15 · **Effort:** M (2–3 days) · **Risk:** medium (a windowed update must equal a fresh build cell for cell; the halo rules are the whole item)

## Gap

`TerrainIsometricRendererComponent.OnCellChanged` (`:312-316`) queues a rebuild for **any** in-bounds `Terrain|Navigation` change, and `OnCellsChangedSignal` is the base default (`TerrainRendererComponent.cs:90-94`): any `Content` change → `QueueRebuild()`, the chunk list ignored. `Rebuild()` (`:371-512`) then clears every level layer (`:407-409`), re-measures water depth over the whole map (`:412`, `:669-709`), re-sorts every mountain height for the summit floor (`:413`, `:636-661`), re-stacks every cell with a `ShowsSide` check per level (`:424-508`, `:605-620`), rebuilds the river polygon (`:509`, `:1046-1079`) and emits `SurfaceRebuilt` (`:511`), which rebuilds the isometric feature renderer's props synchronously (`Drawing.cs:35`, `:44`). One `FillTerrain` on Huge is 10 240 cells × up to five layers, every edit.

The other views already window their edits: the painted view rewrites only the changed chunks' texels plus a one-cell shade halo (`TerrainPaintedRendererComponent.cs:459-484`); the tile view repaints four dual cells (`TerrainTransitionLayerComponent.cs:335-342`); the autotile pack path repaints the affected window (VIEW-08 extends it to the bindings path). No probe measures the block view's edit cost — `terrain_live_relief_probe.gd` covers the relief renderer (`:35-45`), not this one.

## Design

1. **Record what was drawn.** Per bounds cell: `(byte Level, byte Source, Vector2I Frame, bool Side)` in `_stacked[]`, the seabed step in `_bedStep[]` (−1 = none), the elevation used, and a `HashSet<Vector2I> _riverCells`; the full build's loop (`:424-508`) fills them as it sets cells. `_mountainHeights` is kept as a sorted list so the summit floor is the element at the percentile index (`:657-660`) without a full re-sort.
2. **What a cell's drawn state depends on** (the halo rules): its own kind/relief/elevation (`SurfaceLevel`, `:860-866`); the map-wide summit floor; `ShowsSide` of itself and of its −x and −y neighbours (a cell is `Lower` for `(x−1, y)` and `(x, y−1)`, `:607-619`); the seabed step, i.e. distance to land within `SeabedDepth` tiles (`:460-467`; VIEW-05's `CellDistances` on the coast field's live window); river membership (`WaterSourceAtCell == "river"`, `:439-450`).
3. **`UpdateCells(dirty)`** replaces the rebuild when `_hasSurface` and nothing structural changed since the build (bounds, origin, `_tileSetSettings` `:346`, source, `SeabedDepth/SeabedStep/LevelHeight`): (a) update `_mountainHeights` for the dirty cells; if the floor moved, add every recorded cell at `Level ≥ Mountains` whose elevation lies between the old and new floor; (b) grow the set by the −x/−y neighbours and by the seabed window of any cell whose land/water state flipped; (c) recompute `(level, source, frame, side)` and bed step for the set, diff against the record, `SetCell`/`EraseCell` only where different, counting `CellsWrittenLastUpdate`; (d) if river membership changed, `RebuildRivers` from `_riverCells`; (e) recompute `_surfaceExtent` arithmetically from the recorded levels (no per-cell `MapToLocal`, unlike `:417-434`); (f) emit `SurfaceRebuilt` once. `OnCellChanged` adds to `_dirty` and calls `QueueRebuild()`; `PerformQueuedRebuild` runs `UpdateCells` when the record is valid and `Rebuild()` otherwise (edits that arrive while hidden accumulate and are applied on show, via the base's visibility notification, `TerrainRendererComponent.cs:96-101`).
4. **`OnCellsChangedSignal(kind, chunks)`** override: a `Content` change with a chunk list → `UpdateCells` over those chunks' cells ∩ bounds (ENH-01's contract); an empty list (whole map: `LoadCells`/`ClearCells`) → `Rebuild()`. `Gameplay`-only per-cell changes stay filtered (`:314`, ENH-02).
5. `GetLayerDiagnostics()` (`:874-917`) reads the live layers, so it equals a fresh build by construction; the probe asserts it. Docs: `docs/terrain-engine/TerrainIsometricRendererComponent.md` (edit section).

## Guards (fail first)

- `tests/terrain_iso_windowed_edit_probe.gd` (headless; the live-cells shape of `terrain_live_relief_probe.gd:22-80` with a block view and the Kenney sheet): 64×64 live map, one lake, one river, built; snapshot every level layer's and the seabed's `tile_map_data`; count `SurfaceRebuilt` emissions. (a) One inland cell → `rock` + `terrain_relief = 2`: exactly one `SurfaceRebuilt`; `CellsWrittenLastUpdate ≤ 15` (the cell across ≤ 5 levels plus its two side-facing neighbours); every layer equals a twin renderer's fresh `Rebuild()` on the same cells; `GetLayerDiagnostics()` equal. (b) One coastal cell water → land: only cells within `SeabedDepth` of it change on the seabed layer; equality with the twin again. (c) One river cell → grass: `IsoRivers.Polygons.Count` drops by one. **Mutation:** route `OnCellChanged` to `Rebuild()` → `CellsWrittenLastUpdate` equals the map → fails. **Mutation:** drop the −x/−y halo → the neighbour's block-versus-top choice differs from the twin → fails. **Mutation:** skip the summit-floor refresh → a new tallest mountain leaves an old summit tier wrong → equality fails.
- Existing green: `iso_layers`, `terrain_iso_art_probe`, `terrain_iso_river_probe`, `terrain_iso_cliff_probe`, `terrain_surface_height_probe`, `terrain_view_grid_probe`, `terrain_world_iso_*`.
- Pin: `UpdateCells(` and `CellsWrittenLastUpdate` declared in `TerrainIsometricRendererComponent.cs`; the probe carries the behaviour.

## Dependencies / collisions

ENH-01/ENH-02 (landed: chunk lists and per-cell kinds are the inputs). VIEW-05 first (the seabed step comes from `CellDistances` on the live coast window; with the BFS the window cannot be bounded). VIEW-10 first (the stepped build's row loop is the loop that fills the record — one loop, two drivers). ENH-06 (making the isometric feature renderer's `SurfaceRebuilt` rebuild windowed is that item's). ENH-07 (per-group residency builds from the same record). No named collision; the block view is a shipped view and keeps its three investment items (owner's decision, 2026-09-15).

## Out of scope

Prop windowing (ENH-06); batching edits across frames (the base coalescer already coalesces per frame); animating level changes; the summit percentile rule itself.
